About PackSwift

One thoughtful place for the whole journey.

PackSwift is an AI-powered travel planning assistant designed to help travellers plan complete trips without searching across multiple websites.

Our mission

Make complete trip planning feel clear and personal.

We connect destination discovery, likely weather, budget context, local culture, itinerary ideas, and packing preparation in one guided experience.

Our vision

A personal digital travel advisor for every traveller.

Our vision is to provide destination insights, climate analysis, packing guidance, cultural information, and trip planning assistance in one platform.
