Plan, save, and prepare with PackSwift.

Start in three steps

  1. Explore destinations on Home, or ask the Concierge for ideas.
  2. Open Plan Trip and set your route, dates, budget, and travel preferences.
  3. Review your places and itinerary, save your plan, then open My Trips to continue preparing.

Common questions

Can I try planning without an account?

Yes. Explore destinations and preview a plan as a guest. Sign in when you want to save a plan under your account.

Where are my saved plans?

Open My Trips for account plans. Preview plans saved in this browser appear on your Profile under Saved Preview Plans. Device saves do not automatically transfer to another browser.

My Trips · Profile
Are prices and opening hours guaranteed?

Budgets are planning estimates. Check the venue's current hours, admission prices, and travel requirements before departure.

Can PackSwift book my trip?

PackSwift helps you plan and prepare. Complete reservations directly with your chosen provider.

Privacy information

Account details and account-saved trips are stored by PackSwift. Some preview plans and preferences are stored in your browser. AI and destination requests may be sent to the configured travel and AI services. Avoid putting passport numbers, payment details, or other sensitive information in trip notes or chat.

Contact support with questions about your account data.

Using PackSwift

PackSwift provides travel planning guidance and estimates. Availability, prices, weather, visa requirements, and safety conditions can change. Review official sources and provider terms before making travel decisions.
